# Legal Doctrine

## Core Position

MashLab AI / CyphaBlend AI is a neutral private audio-processing tool. The user supplies audio and is responsible for rights.

Required notice:

> Upload audio you own or are authorized to use. MashLab AI helps process and arrange it. Rights to publish or distribute are separate and remain the user's responsibility.

## Product Constraints

- Do not provide music.
- Do not encourage use of copyrighted music.
- Do not include a downloader.
- Do not connect to Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud, Apple Music, or streaming sources.
- Do not include a public sharing hub in the MVP.
- Do not maintain a public catalog of copyrighted songs.
- Do not market the product as a way to remix famous artists or copyrighted hits.
- Do not train on user uploads unless explicit opt-in exists later.
- Do not train on copyrighted music without a rights strategy.

## UI Requirements

The upload workflow, export panel, and legal notice screen must include the required notice or a shortened link to it. Upload actions should use language such as "Choose local audio" rather than "Import from Spotify" or "Find a song."

Recommended supporting copy:

- "Local browser inspection only in this prototype."
- "Engine adapters are pending; no AI mashup has been generated yet."
- "Publishing, distributing, or performing exported material may require separate rights."

## Marketing Restrictions

Avoid claims such as:

- "Remix any hit song."
- "Make viral remixes from famous artists."
- "Download from streaming services."
- "Share your mashups with the world."

Acceptable positioning:

- "Prepare private DJ mashup drafts from audio you are authorized to use."
- "Analyze, align, arrange, and export with human control."
- "A professional workflow for user-supplied audio."
