# Product Vision

## Promise

Two songs in. A DJ-ready mashup out.

MashLab AI / CyphaBlend AI helps DJs and producers prepare private mashup drafts from audio they own or are authorized to use. The product should feel like a serious studio utility: precise, fast, transparent about processing status, and respectful of creative control.

## Product Doctrine

MashLab AI / CyphaBlend AI is a neutral private audio-processing tool.

The app must not provide music, encourage use of copyrighted music, include a downloader, connect to Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud, Apple Music, or any other streaming source, or launch with a public sharing hub. The MVP is local-first and user-supplied-file only.

Required user notice:

> Upload audio you own or are authorized to use. MashLab AI helps process and arrange it. Rights to publish or distribute are separate and remain the user's responsibility.

## MVP User Journey

1. User lands in a professional studio-style workspace.
2. User uploads Track A and Track B from local files.
3. The app inspects safe local metadata: file name, format, size, duration, sample rate, channel count, and waveform summary when browser decoding succeeds.
4. The app shows engine-pending areas for BPM, downbeat, key, phrase, stem, arrangement, and export intelligence.
5. User understands what is implemented now, what is planned, and what legal responsibilities remain theirs.

## Product Principles

- Do not fake AI completion.
- Keep legal boundaries visible without making the tool hostile.
- Design for DJs who need timing, phrasing, gain, key, and export clarity.
- Keep user files private by default.
- Do not train on uploads unless an explicit opt-in program exists later.
- Do not train on copyrighted music without a rights strategy.
- Do not use examples based on copyrighted songs or famous artists.

## Future Experience

The finished product should analyze two files, separate stems, align beat grids, estimate key compatibility, propose multiple mashup drafts, allow detailed human edits, and export DJ-safe masters and stems.
