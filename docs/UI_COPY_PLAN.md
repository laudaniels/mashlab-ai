# UI Copy Plan

## Required Notice

Upload audio you own or are authorized to use. MashLab AI helps process and arrange it. Rights to publish or distribute are separate and remain the user's responsibility.

## Primary Labels

- "Choose local audio"
- "Track A"
- "Track B"
- "Local metadata"
- "Analysis coming next"
- "Engine pending"
- "Export locked until processing engines are connected"

## Avoided Language

- "Import from Spotify"
- "Download from YouTube"
- "Remix any hit song"
- "Make viral remixes from famous artists"
- "Publish to public hub"

## Tone

The UI should be direct and professional. It should explain status and rights clearly without sounding accusatory or theatrical.
